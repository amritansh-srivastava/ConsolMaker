Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. How long does it take to build a website?

The timeline depends on the project's complexity. A standard business website typically takes 2–4 weeks, while larger custom websites or e-commerce platforms may require 4–8 weeks or more.

2. Will my website be mobile-friendly?

Yes. Every website we develop is fully responsive, ensuring it looks and performs seamlessly on desktops, tablets, and smartphones.

3. Do you provide SEO services?

Absolutely. We build websites with SEO best practices in mind and also offer comprehensive SEO services, including keyword research, on-page optimization, technical SEO, and local SEO to improve your online visibility.

4. Can I update my website after it's launched?

Yes. If your website includes a Content Management System (CMS), you'll be able to update text, images, and other content yourself. We also offer ongoing maintenance and support if you'd prefer us to handle updates.

5. How much does a website cost?

The cost depends on your requirements, such as the number of pages, features, design complexity, and integrations. Contact us for a free consultation, and we'll provide a customized quote based on your needs.
